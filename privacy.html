Privacy policy

Last updated 18 October 2026.

This policy covers the Etc. Studios Content Studio ("the app"), a private desktop app used only by Etc. Studios to manage its own social media accounts.

What the app accesses

With the account owner's permission (given through each platform's own sign-in screen), the app accesses only the Etc. Studios accounts on YouTube (Google), TikTok and Instagram (Meta): basic profile information, our own videos and posts, their statistics, and comments left on them. It uploads videos and captions that Etc. Studios made.

How it is used

Only to publish Etc. Studios content, schedule posts, reply to and moderate comments on our own posts, and measure how our videos perform. We do not sell, rent or share this data with anyone, and do not use it for advertising.

Where it is kept

Access keys and statistics are stored only on Etc. Studios' own computer, in a folder only the app can read. Nothing is stored on third-party servers by us. Statistics are kept as long as they are useful for planning videos and can be deleted at any time.

Comments and viewers

When the app reads public comments on our videos, it sees what the platform shows any viewer (name and comment text). We use this only to read and answer comments on our own channel.

YouTube and Google

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using our YouTube features you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be removed at any time at Google security settings. The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Each platform lets the account owner remove the app's access at any time from that platform's settings (Google: Third-party connections; TikTok: Manage app permissions; Instagram: Apps and websites). Removing access stops all data use.
